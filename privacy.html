Privacy Policy & Terms

1. Your Privacy

We respect your privacy. When you fill out a form on our site (to order eggs or say hello), we receive your name and email address.

  • We only use your email to reply to your questions or orders.
  • We never sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • We do not store your data on a public server; it is sent directly to our email inbox.

2. Donations & Payments

The Flocking Reed's Farm is a private homestead/business, not a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.

Please Note: Any funds sent via CashApp, Venmo, or PayPal are considered personal gifts or payments for goods (eggs/produce). They are not tax-deductible.

3. Farm Products

Our eggs and produce are sold in accordance with New Hampshire state regulations for small farm stands. Products are sold "as-is" and are fresh from the farm. Please wash all produce before eating.
