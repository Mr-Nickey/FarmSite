Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 22, 2026

🔐 Google User Data & Authentication

The Flocking Reed's Farm offers the option to log in using Google ("Sign in with Google"). If you choose to use this feature, we access the following limited information:

  • Email Address: Used solely to create your unique user account and communicate regarding orders or support.
  • Name & Profile Picture: Used to display your avatar and nickname on your Dashboard and in Community comments.
  • OpenID Identifier: Used to verify your identity securely without storing your password.

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your Google User Data with third parties. This data is used strictly for authentication and account management purposes on this website.

Information We Collect

1. Account Information: When you register (via email or Google), we store your name, email, and hashed password (if applicable). We do not see or store your raw Google password.

2. Transaction Data: If you donate or purchase merch, payments are processed by third-party providers (Stripe, PayPal, Venmo). We do not store your credit card information on our servers.

3. Usage Data & Cookies: We use essential cookies to keep you logged in. We may use privacy-focused analytics (like Cloudflare or GoatCounter) to count visitors, but we do not use invasive cross-site tracking pixels.

4. Farm Stand Security: For the safety of our property and livestock, 24/7 Video Surveillance is in use at the physical farm stand location in Grafton, NH. These recordings are stored locally and are not uploaded publicly unless required for legal or security reasons.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and manage your access to "The Roost" (our community blog).
  • To process merchandise orders via our print-on-demand partner (Printify).
  • To send you transactional emails (order confirmations, password resets).
  • To respond to your inquiries via our support inbox.

Third-Party Sharing

We are a simple homestead, not a data broker. We only share data when necessary to fulfill a service:

  • Printify: If you buy merch, we share your shipping address with Printify to fulfill the order.
  • Stripe/PayPal: To process payments securely.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law enforcement or to protect the safety of our farm and family.

Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and data at any time. To exercise this right, please contact us via the form on our homepage or email support@flockingreeds.farm.
