Legal Stuff & Farm Safety

We keep it simple, but we gotta cover the bases.

🚜 Farm Safety & Liability

The Flocking Reed's Farm is a working homestead.

While our Farm Stand is located safely near the road, please adhere to the following for your safety:

  • Stay in Designated Areas: Please stick to the driveway and Farm Stand area. The animals live "out back" and their enclosures are private property.
  • Uneven Ground: This is a farm, not a grocery store. Watch your step for rocks, uneven dirt, or ice in the winter.
  • Security Monitoring: For the safety of our property and animals, 24/7 Video Surveillance is in use around the farm stand and driveway.

🥚 Egg Safety & Handling

We sell Unwashed, Bloom-Intact Eggs. This means the natural protective coating is left on the egg to preserve freshness.

Customer Responsibilities (NH RSA 428):

  • Wash Before Use: Please wash eggs with warm water immediately before cooking.
  • Keep Refrigerated: To maintain freshness and safety, keep eggs refrigerated at or below 45°F.
  • Consumption: Consuming raw or undercooked eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness.

Our eggs are candled for cracks and quality, but are not USDA graded. They are sold as "Fresh / Not Sized."
