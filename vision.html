Growing Where We Are Planted

Honoring the land. Respecting the roots. Building a future right here.

1. A Partnership of Stewardship

We are incredibly lucky to rent from a landlady who supports our dream. While most renters are limited to indoor plants, we have been given the rare freedom to farm this beautiful land.

Our vision isn't temporary. We treat this property with the love and care of a forever home. By investing in the soil and building lasting infrastructure, we are honoring her trust in us and planting deep roots for our family's future.

2. Current Goals

🐐

The Dairy Project (Infrastructure Phase)

We have officially received the green light to add livestock! Our goal is to bring two dairy goats to the farm to produce milk, cheese, and soap. Currently, we have a blank slate—no fences, no barn. We are fundraising to build the secure fencing and shelter required to bring them home safely. This is our biggest infrastructure project yet.

🪿

The Forever Coop

Our two geese are the guardians of the flock. Right now, they reside in a temporary pallet shelter. It has served us well, but they deserve a permanent home. We are upgrading them to a reinforced, insulated 'Goose Fortress' designed to withstand New Hampshire winters and keep them safe from predators for years to come.

🥕

Community Food Security

The ultimate goal is a farm that feeds the neighborhood. From fresh eggs at the stand to a summer garden full of veggies, we want locals to have access to real, healthy food right down the road.

3. Fueling the Farm

We operate on a 'Content Loop.' We film our journey for YouTube, and the support from viewers like you goes directly back into the soil and the animals.

Sustaining the Flock 💙

Construction is exciting, but consistency is key. Your support covers the daily essentials: high-quality grain, fresh bedding, and vet bills. You keep the bellies full and the animals happy while we build.

Building the Future ☀️

These funds tackle the big projects. They buy the rolls of fencing for the goats, the lumber for the Forever Coop, and the heavy-duty supplies that turn a backyard into a homestead.

"We aren't just building a farm; we are building a home."
