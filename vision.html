The 2026 Roadmap

We build slowly, we use what we have, and we adapt. Here is our plan for the year, from the first thaw of spring to the first snow of winter.

PHASE 1

🌷 Spring Awakening

🐐

The Clean-Up Crew (Goats)

Our goal this spring is to bring home our first goats! Their primary job will be sustainability: clearing the brush, shrubs, and overgrowth that takes over the land. Long-term, they will provide milk and cheese for the community, but for now, they are the cutest landscaping crew in Grafton.

🍅

Sammie's Garden Upgrade

Sammie is the first to admit that gardening is a "trial and error" process! Last year was tough, but we are trying again with tomatoes, greens, potatoes, and maybe even corn.

To extend the season, we are saving up for a Poly Tunnel (a sturdy metal frame or kit) that can actually withstand a NH winter. We are also building a DIY watering system, using recycled PEX pipe to run water from our rain barrels directly to the plants.

PHASE 2

☀️ Summer Build

🐣

The Heritage Flock (Strictly No-Kill!)

We spoil our animals. We don't eat our chickens; we only eat the eggs. In fact, every coop has a heating lamp because we believe happy, warm chickens lay the best tasting eggs.

Update: The first generation has hatched! We have a new batch of chicks (hatched in late December) growing in the brooder right now. By summer, they will need a new "Forever Run"—a roofed, reinforced structure where they can scratch in the dirt safely.

🪿

The Goose Fortress

Our geese need more room. We plan to build one large, permanent coop using real lumber and cinderblocks. This isn't just a shelter; it's a winter bunker. We want to give them plenty of space to hunker down comfortably together when those big snowstorms roll in.

PHASE 3

❄️ "Winter is Coming"

🌨️

The Long Prep

In New England, you don't wait for snow to get ready. The fall is for stocking up.

  • Bulking Up: Stacking hay high for the new goats.
  • Season Extension: Using the new Poly Tunnel to keep greens growing for the animals well into the frost.
  • Staying Warm: Checking all the heat lamps to ensure everyone stays cozy when the temperature drops.

Help Us Build It

We build with recycled materials whenever we can, but some things (like fencing, the metal tunnel frame, and roof panels) need to be bought. Your support buys the screws, the plastic, and the feed that makes this possible.
