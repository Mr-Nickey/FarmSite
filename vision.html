Growing Where We Are Planted

Honoring the land. Respecting the roots. Building a future right here.

1. A Partnership of Stewardship

We are incredibly lucky to rent from an amazing landlady who supports our dream. Most renters can't dig gardens or build pallet chicken coops, but she has graciously given us the freedom to farm this beautiful land.

Our vision isn't about leaving; it's about treating this property with the love and care of a forever home. By investing in the soil and infrastructure here, we honor her trust in us and build a sustainable future for our family.

2. Current Goals

🐐

The Dairy Goal (Goats)

Thanks to our landlord's support, we finally have the green light to expand the farm. Our biggest dream is to bring two dairy goats to the farm.

We are currently fundraising to build the secure fencing and shelter required to do this responsibly. This step will allow us to produce fresh milk, cheese, and soap for our family and the community.

🪿

The Goose Coop Rebuild

Our geese are the guardians of the flock. We are upgrading their current shelter to a reinforced, insulated "Goose Fortress" to keep them safe from predators and warm during the Grafton winters.

🥕

Community Food Security

The ultimate goal is a farm that feeds the neighborhood. From fresh eggs at the stand to a summer garden full of veggies, we want locals to have access to real, healthy food right down the road.

3. How Your Support Helps

We operate on a "Content Loop." We film our journey for YouTube, and the support from viewers like you goes directly back into the infrastructure.

Small Donations 💙

Cover feed, bedding, and vet bills. You keep the animals fed and happy.

Large Investments 🌟

Build the goat fencing, coop rebuilds and repairs, and the garden tunnels. You build the legacy.
