## 🐓 About Our Homestead

Flocking Reeds Farm is built on love, hard work, and deep respect for animal life. Tucked into the hills of Grafton, NH, owners **Sammie & Nicky** care for over 60 pasture-raised laying chickens, Coturnix quail, watch geese, ducks, rabbits, and rescue critters.

### 💛 100% No-Kill Philosophy
Unlike commercial farms, we operate on a strict **100% no-kill policy**. We do not cull or slaughter our animals. Every bird and animal lives out its natural life here as a permanent member of our homestead family.

### 🤝 "Pay What You Can" Neighbor Promise
We believe everyone in our rural community deserves access to fresh, wholesome food. If a neighbor or family is struggling financially, they are welcome to take a dozen eggs from our stand and leave whatever they can afford — even if it's just a dollar in pennies.

## 💻 Tech Stack & Site Architecture

This static web application is designed for lightning-fast performance, full mobile responsiveness, and high accessibility across all viewports.

* **Frontend:** React 18, TypeScript, Vite
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Custom Harvest Gold & Farm Green theme with dark mode support)
* **Icons:** Lucide React & Custom SVG brand icons
* **Forms:** Web3Forms API integration with built-in spam protection and inquiry topic routing
* **SEO & Social Sharing:** Dynamic OpenGraph social cards, Schema.org JSON-LD structured data, and meta tag management
* **Hosting:** Static web application hosted via Cloudflare Pages connected to our GitHub repository.
